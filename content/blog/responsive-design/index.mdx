Responsive design makes a website comfortable to use on phones, tablets, and
desktop screens. It is not only about making things smaller; it is about keeping
content readable and actions easy to reach.

Good spacing, flexible layouts, and readable font sizes can make a simple page
feel much more polished. These details help visitors focus on the content
instead of fighting the layout.

I try to test my pages at different screen sizes so I can catch problems early
and keep the experience consistent.
